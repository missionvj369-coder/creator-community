# Human Within · Independent experiences

The original fifteen products are separate, always-open experiences. A suggested emotional order creates a coherent flow without a daily plan, checklist or completion quota. The three main tabs are Experiences, Ebooks and Your space.

Entry remains title reveal → three welcome films → five-minute Still Room practice → the original automatic calming sequence → the requested app page. Audible media requires a browser gesture. Leaving meditation gently still passes through the calming sequence; bedtime can remain dark until an intentional return.

Rewire opens the thirty-artwork wallpaper studio, and Clarity includes A World Within as its affirmation companion.

## Product transitions

The eight native product entrances provide Overview, Collection/Inside/Artworks, and Where next navigation, with a distinct illustrated entrance and a short orientation. The seven reading worlds open directly into their immersive welcomes and keep their collections within reach. Each has a previous and next invitation in the emotional order. A reading or listening completion opens a quiet pause screen, with the next item in the same collection as the primary choice where applicable. A separate invitation leads to the following product. Immersive worlds show the pause screen after Leave gently; unloading the world stops its media. Returning is always an explicit choice.

Brief CSS entrance fades keep navigation responsive without snapshot overlays. All interface animation respects both the system and app reduced-motion setting. The five-chapter index remains available while scrolling and identifies the current section.

## Data

The frontend remains browser-local. Existing notes, bookmarks, ebook positions, personal threads, sound history and backup/restore remain. Retired daily-plan completion counts carry into independent course progress; an unfinished daily draft and its reflection remain in the backup. Old daily URLs and activity links resolve to their independent product. The old ledger is retained only for compatibility and honest lifetime totals; no daily planner is rendered or advanced.

Database sync, accounts and paid checkout remain deferred. No QA fixtures are included in the deployed public directory.

Exiting a world removes its animation, sound and listeners; hidden tabs pause artwork and sound. Reduced motion and a still-art fallback are supported.
