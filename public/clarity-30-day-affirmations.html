The science behind the practice

These 60 original statements are prompts for intentional behavior, paired with small actions. They are not a clinically validated treatment or a promise to rewire your brain in 30 days. Here, “the matrix” means everyday autopilot, comparison, and attention traps.

Self-affirmation research often asks people to reflect on meaningful personal values—not simply repeat positive slogans. One brain-imaging study found activity in regions associated with self-related processing and valuation during value reflection. This does not prove that these particular sentences change the brain. Read the study ↗

Habit research supports repeating a behavior in a stable context. The morning and bedtime cues help organize practice; there is no established special “rewiring window” at those times. Habit formation varies between people and behaviors. Thirty days is a practice structure, not a biological deadline. Read the habit review ↗
