SCIENCE · ART · EVERYDAY CARE

A thread, not a test.

This is an original work of interactive art and visual learning. Scientific observations, mathematical forms, and reflective metaphors are identified in each world’s insight.

Shapes, colors, timescales, and transitions are illustrative. They are not scans, medical measurements, a model of evolution, or predictions about you. Suggested actions are invitations to think and help; no health effect is promised.

Sound is optional. The ambient tones have no claimed therapeutic frequency. Nothing is sent or recorded by this experience. Source links open external websites only when selected.

Controls: ←/→ previous or next world · Space pause · Q quiet view · Escape close. Reduced-motion settings begin in manual mode. The chapter map provides text access to every insight.

For a group: explore a world, read its insight, and discuss one practical action. Different reactions are welcome; this experience gives no score or personality assessment.
