Science and symbolism

Each animated sculpture illustrates an established idea or a human experience. These are poetic visual models, not laboratory measurements, medical simulations, or scans. The original five forms and their particle-motion style are retained.

  • STARS. Hydrogen formed early in the universe; many heavier elements were formed through stellar processes. NASA.
  • DNA. DNA stores hereditary information. Development also depends on environment and cellular processes. NHGRI.
  • BODY, HEART & BREATH. Circulation and gas exchange help sustain life. Rhythms shown are illustrative, not medical feedback. MedlinePlus.
  • MIND, SENSES & MEMORY. Perception and learning involve dynamic networks in the nervous system. NINDS.
  • CELL. Cells maintain boundaries and regulate processes essential to life. NHGRI.
  • BONDS & CULTURE. Social connection, communication and learning link human lives across generations. Connections in the sculpture are symbolic.
  • NATURE & ONENESS. Human survival depends on living ecosystems. Our shared biological heritage does not erase the richness of human differences.

No diagnostic, psychological, medical or health claims are made. The artwork is designed for exploration and reflection.
