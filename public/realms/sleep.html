Twenty Minutes Down: Sleep

Nothing to write. Nothing to choose. Twenty minutes of guided settling, then an original instrumental score that fades to silence by minute forty.

Lie down, keep the volume just audible, and close your eyes whenever you like. Follow the recorded voice, or simply rest. Breathe at your own comfortable pace. Use this practice when you can rest safely.

About this practice

A recorded voice guides twenty minutes of settling. Original stereo music then fades gently over twenty more minutes. Breathe comfortably, keep your eyes open if you prefer, and pause whenever you wish. These timings are design choices. The app does not measure sleep or promise a sleep stage.
