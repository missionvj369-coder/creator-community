Before you had a name, life was already moving toward you. Then one day, without choosing it, you arrived. A body. A family. A world. A thousand small moments that slowly became your life.

This is not a lesson about what people do. It is a visual passage through what it feels like to be human—from before birth to the mystery of what remains after death. Move at your own pace. Let a scene remind you of something.
