# Human Within — app and ebook review

Review completed 30 October 2026 (India time). Revision 6.1.2.

## Verdict

Keep all six ebooks. The collection has useful material and a coherent visual identity. The largest remaining book task is a developmental edit of **The Human Journey**, particularly chronology and the repeated conclusions in Part III. The other books need focused maintenance rather than complete rewrites.

The two missing library cover titles are fixed. The artwork for The Human Journey and Your Child’s Growing Mind did not contain their separate text layers. The library now renders responsive, accessible cover typography. The other four covers retain their existing titles. All six reader opening screens were also inspected.

This is an editorial and functional review with selected primary-source checks. It is not an independent clinical review, a systematic review of every scientific assertion, a full accessibility certification, or a guarantee of behavior on every phone.

### The Human Journey: corrections made

- Chapter 31 already places Tara at age one when the fellowship offer arrives. Chapter 32’s question about whether she would have been born was changed to whether she would have gone with Mira; the later recollection in Chapter 37 now agrees.
- “A baby of one” became “one year old”; “unrealiztic” became “unrealistic.”
- Chapter 48 no longer says Tara had spoken for an hour after a seven-minute council speaking slot.
- Chapter 1 now describes broad agreement for hydrogen and helium while acknowledging the unsettled lithium detail, rather than claiming an exact match for every light element. The Particle Data Group review explicitly discusses the lithium discrepancy [2].
- Chapter 2’s Andromeda passage now says its light left before our own species existed. “Before there were any people” was too broad given evidence for early Homo about 2.8 million years ago [3].

### The Human Journey: developmental edit still needed

Give the later family scenes explicit year or age cues. One passage presents Mira at 57 and Tara at 29; another sequence begins with Tara at nine and later describes Mira turning 40. Time may pass within that sequence, so changing an age without a chronology map would introduce another error. Build the map before editing those numbers.

Part III returns repeatedly to maps, committees, collective responsibility and model revisions. Consolidate repeated explanations and let concrete choices carry more of the argument. Chapters 44, 48 and 49 each have the weight of an ending; assign them different functions so the final ending feels earned. The existing book has 49 numbered chapters and around 54,200 words. More words alone would not solve these issues.

### Parenting: exact reference correction

Source [23] is Leonard et al. (2021), **Children Persist Less When Adults Take Over**, *Child Development*, **92(4), 1325–1336**, DOI **10.1111/cdev.13492** [1]. The previous entry used the wrong title and page range. All three page representations now agree, and the same correction is included in the offline pack.

The shared-reading review’s approximate 1–6 age range describes average ages across studies, rather than a strict range containing every child. Clarify that wording on page 10 and in the corresponding source description together. This is a precision improvement, not a reason to replace the discovery.

## App content reviewed and corrected

The wider content pass covered the 17 non-ebook JSON arrays (570 entries), the 55 Journey collection chapters, all 105 Escape scenarios, the six realm cue/scene tables, all 99 Frequency mappings, the 99 A World Within affirmations and the wallpaper catalogue. These are content-review counts; the functional catalogue separately contains 375 adapted reading units across the original 15 products. This review did not transcribe or independently validate every spoken line in every attached video.

Changes made:

- Restored the two missing library cover titles; verified no horizontal overflow at 320 px and 390 px iframe widths. Screen-reader link names retain the full book title; decorative cover text is hidden from duplicate announcement.
- Corrected 15 concrete extraction, spelling or source-attribution errors across Awakening and One God. Removed pasted cycle headings from two Quran fields, repaired split words, replaced three misattributed sayings with identified reflective paraphrases, and corrected four mistaken verse references [4–10].
- Replaced the 21 Sound Reset session names that implied specific physiological mechanisms with plain sensory names. Band descriptions now describe the generated audio pulse, and the note distinguishes a tone pattern from a measured brain state or a medical outcome. Tone frequencies and session lengths remain the same. Research is mixed and parameter-dependent; the cited studies do not validate this app’s former dopamine, eye-muscle or “neural erasure” labels [11–12].
- Added a visibility listener to pause a running sound session when the document becomes hidden. Previously its timer stopped while its generated sound could continue. Returning to the page requires an intentional Resume. Cleanup removes the listener and stops the session.
- Removed the unsupported explanation that a once-per-day limit lets the nervous system consolidate; the daily unlock behavior is unchanged.
- Replaced three grounding statements that asserted a physiological reset, universal safety or a prescribed final emotional state with optional, concrete noticing language.
- Replaced two health-healing affirmations with achievable statements about rest and care.
- Corrected the separate sleep realm’s catalogue duration to **40 minutes: 20 guided, 20 fading**. Its evidence copy now describes the laboratory conditions and states that this app does not sense whether someone is asleep. This realm is separate from The Still Room’s five- and twenty-minute presets.
- Rebuilt the three changed offline ebook packs and updated their byte metadata. The packaging helper now accepts explicit book IDs and refuses to replace an existing split download with an oversized ZIP.

The Frequency mapping has two entries marked as studied tone pairs. Those source links do not establish effectiveness for all 99 custom scenarios, or for this particular implementation. Preserve that distinction in future marketing.

### Highest-priority remaining app content work

Awakening still has 264 tradition lines without complete verse-and-translation attribution. The 15 corrections above are a bounded set, not a certification of the remaining quotations. Before a wider launch, give every line a verifiable source and translation, or label it clearly as an original reflection or paraphrase. Distinct traditions should retain their own context; a shared theme does not make their theology interchangeable.

Improve navigation by problem and purpose: a brief discovery, help winding down, understanding a child, learning about the body, practising an AI task, or reading a longer human story. The collection is stronger when each book has a clear job. Adding more unrelated entries would not resolve that need.

## Reproduce the technical checks

From the checkout:

```sh
npm test
npm run verify:books
```

The controlled browser fixtures live under `scripts/browser-qa`. Copy a fixture into `public` only for internal QA and remove it before building. The static release must not contain `qa-*.html`.
