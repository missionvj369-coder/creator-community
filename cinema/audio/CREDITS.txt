HUMAN WITHIN — ORIGINAL SOUND LIBRARY

Twenty-two original instrumental arrangements, stereo sound design, four soft transition cues, twenty original spoken welcomes and nine recorded opening/grounding cues created for Human Within, 2026. A twenty-minute night score fades gradually to silence after the existing twenty-minute narration. The compositional source and reproducible rendering scripts are included with the app source.

Scores: sampled piano, harp, strings, cello, marimba and related instruments rendered through FluidSynth, with original MIDI note arrangements, humanised timing, stereo positioning, room reflections and equal-power loop joins. Intermediate masters: 48 kHz stereo, 24-bit PCM. App scores: 48 kHz stereo AAC at 256 kb/s, matched to a -23 LUFS target and -2 dBTP limit. These are production settings, not a promise about the listener's device or hearing experience.

Instrument samples: Fluid (R3) SoundFont, Copyright (c) 2000–2002, 2008 Frank Wen and contributors, MIT licence. The full upstream copyright/licence is in FluidR3-COPYRIGHT.txt. Distribution source: https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/f/fluid-soundfont/

Spoken welcomes: original text, rendered locally with Kokoro-82M / kokoro-onnx, preset af_heart, also used in the existing Still Room. Original model: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0). Runtime: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx (MIT). The model's original voice sample rate is 24 kHz; app files use 48 kHz AAC for compatible playback. No voice cloning or user recordings are used.

The existing Still Room narration and original welcome films retain their original authorship. Their content and timing are preserved. The welcome film soundtrack has matched dialogue loudness, gentle compression and a low-frequency rumble filter; its video stream is unchanged. The app reuses the continuous film for its three messages; individual source films are retained with the app source. Music is optional, volume is adjustable, and the frequency/tone tools retain their own tone parameters. No track is a clinical treatment or a validated healing frequency.

Discovery rooms: Curiosity (Wonder Engine), Invisible (The Unseen), and Atelier (The Creation Field) are three additional original compositions using sample-free additive instruments, shaped envelopes and stereo room reflections. Reproduce with scripts/render-discovery-scores.py. Optional touch notes use the same global mute and effects settings. Exported artwork loops are silent.
