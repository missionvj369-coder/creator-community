THE STILL ROOM
A gentle arrival · 5 minutes

00:00 — Welcome to the Still Room
Welcome to the Still Room. Find a position you can stay in comfortably. For these few minutes, nothing needs to be finished.

00:24 — Let the surface hold you
Notice the support beneath you. The chair, the bed, or the ground can take some of your weight. You can keep your eyes open.

00:45 — An ordinary breath
Notice one ordinary breath. Let it arrive without pulling it deeper. Let it leave without pushing. There is no breath to hold.

01:05 — A rhythm that fits you
If it feels easy, let the breath become a little slower. Keep it small and comfortable. Your own rhythm is always enough.

01:28 — Nothing to force
You do not need to follow the light or any count. If you feel uncomfortable, return to your usual breathing, or pause whenever you wish.

01:57 — You can let the view go
Let your breathing keep its own rhythm now. If it feels comfortable, soften your gaze, or close your eyes. There is nothing more you need to watch.

02:23 — Begin with the feet
Bring a little attention to your feet. Perhaps there is warmth, pressure, or very little to notice. There is no special feeling to find.

02:42 — Let the legs rest
Notice your lower legs and your thighs. You can leave them as they are. If it is comfortable, let the surface beneath take their weight.

03:01 — Support beneath your body
Notice the hips and the back. There may be points of contact with the surface beneath you. Nothing needs to be pressed down or held still.

03:20 — Hands with nothing to carry
Let attention reach the hands and arms. They can rest wherever they are comfortable. For this moment, there is nothing they need to carry.

03:38 — A little less effort
Notice the shoulders, the jaw, and the space around the eyes. Perhaps a little effort can soften. If it cannot, that is all right too.

03:58 — The body as a whole
You may notice the body as a whole, breathing in its own rhythm. Rest with a sound, a point of contact, or simply this moment.

04:18 — Thoughts can come and go
If thoughts arrive, you have not done anything wrong. Let them come and go. You can return gently to the feeling of support beneath you.

04:40 — Come back gently
If you are ready, notice the room around you. Feel your hands and feet. Open your eyes whenever you wish. Take this pace with you.
