This is a reflective journey with practical actions. It invites an ethical sense of oneness; it does not establish a shared consciousness or guarantee a change in behaviour.

The prompts favour asking over assuming, specific actions over vague intentions, and voluntary participation. Your action count is a personal record, not a measure of compassion.

Research context: Eyal, Steffel & Epley (2018): getting another person’s perspective through conversation; Böckler et al. (2018): different mental trainings and prosocial behaviour. These studies did not test this tool.
